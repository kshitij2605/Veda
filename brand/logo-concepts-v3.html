Five takes on the watching eye

N1 / Constellation iris Sanjaya's divya-drishti — the divine eye whose iris is Veda's constellation. "Niriksha watches Veda."
N2 / Eye of nodes The eye drawn the same way Veda's V is drawn — a closed walk through six nodes, pupil at the center.
N3 / Arrowhead pupil Arjuna distilled — the eye whose pupil is the arrowhead. He saw nothing but the eye; the eye holds the arrow.
N4 / Feather eye The eye of Krishna's peacock feather — the ornament that watched over the Mahabharata.
N5 / Trinetra The vertical third eye — inner, divine seeing. Quietest and most symbolic of the set.
