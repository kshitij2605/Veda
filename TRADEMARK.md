The Veda Authors. This policy explains how you may and may not use them.

> Software licenses (AGPL / FSL / commercial) cover the **code**. They do **not** grant any
> right to use the Marks. Trademark is a separate, retained right — it's what stops someone
> from taking the code and reselling it *under our name*.
>
> **Before publishing:** replace `The Veda Authors` with the legal owner and consider
> **registering the marks** in your primary jurisdiction(s). Not legal advice.

## You MAY (no permission needed)
- Use the Marks in **plain text to refer to the project** accurately — "built with Veda",
  "compatible with Veda", "a Niriksha plugin", blog posts, talks, comparisons.
- State, truthfully, that your product **integrates with or uses** Veda/Niriksha.
- Use the unmodified logos to link to the official project.

## You MAY NOT (without written permission)
- Use the Marks (or confusingly similar names) **in the name of your own product, service,
  company, domain, or a fork** — e.g. "VedaCloud", "Veda Pro", "Niriksha+", "Veda-as-a-Service".
- Use the Marks in a way that **implies endorsement, affiliation, or official status** that
  doesn't exist.
- Modify the logos, or use them as your own brand/app icon.
- Use the Marks for a **fork or redistribution** in a way that could confuse users about the
  source — a fork must be clearly renamed and must not present itself as official Veda/Niriksha.

## Forks and redistribution
You are free (under the code licenses) to fork and redistribute the code. If you do, you
must **use a different name and branding** for your distribution and remove the Marks from
anything that could imply it is the official project. (Reference: Redis → Valkey, where the
fork could take the code but not the "Redis" name.)
